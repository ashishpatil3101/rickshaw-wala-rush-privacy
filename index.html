Last updated: [DATE]

Rickshaw Wala Rush ("the app") is developed by Vaibhav Patil ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, approximate location based on your IP address, and how you interact with ads, in order to show and measure ads. Learn more in Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads. You can reset or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Game progress

Your coins, unlocked cities/autos, upgrades and stats are saved only on your device (local storage). Nothing is sent to me or stored on any server. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes this save permanently.

What the app does not collect

The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no online multiplayer. It does not collect your contacts, photos, files, precise location or any other personal information.

Children

Rickshaw Wala Rush is not directed at children under 13.
